Features

Everything Window Switcher can do.

It does one job: get you to the window you want in one or two keys. Everything here helps with that, and none of it gets in the way.

Open it

One shortcut, and it waits for you.

⌥

⌥⇥ that waits

Press ⌥⇥ and let go. The switcher stays open until you press a key, so there's nothing to hold down.

⌥⇥ that waits →
⌘

Replace ⌘⇥

Make Command-Tab open Window Switcher instead. The macOS switcher comes back whenever Window Switcher quits.

Replace ⌘⇥ →
⚡

Instant open

Show the switcher at once from the last window list, then update it a moment later. Off by default.

Instant open →
◌

Quiet in the menu bar

Nothing runs until you press the shortcut. Hide the icon, launch at login, and get told about updates, never surprised by them.

Quiet in the menu bar →

Press a key

A key for every app and every window.

Automatic keys

Every app gets a key from the start, usually its first letter, and keeps it from one day to the next.

Automatic keys →
✎

Your own keys

Put the apps you use most on the keys you want. Automatic keys move out of their way.

Your own keys →
⇥

Two keys to any window

The app's key, then the window's letter. Letters follow the order windows opened, so they don't shuffle.

Two keys to any window →
★

A window's own key

Give the window you always want a single key, matched by its title. One press and you're there.

A window's own key →

Arrows and mouse

Rather not use letters? Arrow keys and ↩ work, and so do hover and click.

Arrows and mouse →

Launch or reopen

An app with a key opens even if it isn't running. A running app with no windows opens a new one.
